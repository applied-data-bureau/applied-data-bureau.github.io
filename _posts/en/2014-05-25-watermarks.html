Business constraints required visible watermarks on product images. The focus here is technical implementation, not legal or ethical debate.

Initial Requirements

1. Make unauthorized reuse difficult.
2. Keep watermark visible on both light and dark backgrounds.
3. Preserve text readability in images.
4. Support batch processing without Photoshop/manual GUI work.

Watermark Design

A single semi-transparent caption was not enough. White text disappears on light backgrounds, dark text disappears on dark ones.

Adding a shadow gave much better contrast across mixed backgrounds.

To improve protection, the final step was tiled watermarking across the whole image, not one centered logo.

Implementation

The solution was built with ImageMagick in Perl. One script generated watermark assets, another applied them to source images in batch mode.

use Image::Magick;
# create transparent canvas, render text, blur, rotate,
# tile watermark layer, composite over source image,
# crop to original bounds, save JPEG with target quality
