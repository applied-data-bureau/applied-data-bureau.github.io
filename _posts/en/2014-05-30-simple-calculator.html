--- layout: post title: "Simple JavaScript Calculator" type: post excerpt: "A sales-side calculator that lets clients reduce prepayment and redistribute payments across months with immediate recalculation." ---

For SEO promotion services, clients often face a relatively high upfront payment. The idea was to make pricing structure transparent and interactive directly on the page.

The calculator lets a client select query volume, choose currency units, and adjust prepayment with a slider.

What it does

  • calculates base monthly plan by service tier
  • recalculates month values when prepayment changes
  • redistributes difference non-linearly across months
  • updates everything instantly in the browser

Implementation uses jQuery/jQuery UI controls and plain JavaScript logic.
