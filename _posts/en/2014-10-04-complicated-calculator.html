This calculator automated a complicated ceiling-sales pricing workflow where manual calculations could take up to 30 minutes per client.

The model had many dependent parameters and discount interactions, so manual work was slow and error-prone.

Why web instead of Excel

The client initially considered Excel. I proposed a web version to avoid version/install issues, centralize logic, and deliver instant recalculation for sales agents.

Frontend stack: JavaScript computation + Bootstrap UI.

The final solution reduced estimation time, lowered onboarding complexity, and improved consistency of sales quotes.
