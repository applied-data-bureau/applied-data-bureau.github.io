--- layout: post title: "Weather Archive for Selected Patagonia Summits" type: post excerpt: "Automating weather data collection from mountain-forecast pages into CSV datasets and chart views for Patagonia peaks." ---

A friend asked to monitor several mountain-forecast pages with identical structure and store daily weather snapshots in CSV.

The parser collected issue date/time and core indicators for the nearest day windows (am/pm/night): summary, wind, snow, high/low temperature, chill, and freezing level.

The result was a reusable archive with visual previews and interactive chart blocks per summit.

Tracked Summits

  • Monte Burney
  • Monte Darwin
  • Lautaro
  • Cerro Arenales
  • San Lorenzo Patagonia
  • Monte San Valentin
