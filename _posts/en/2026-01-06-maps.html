I will build a professional interactive map for your business on leading mapping platforms: Yandex, Leaflet, OSM, Mapbox, Google, or Baidu. I select the best stack based on your business logic, audience, and region.

My goal is to deliver a map that helps sales, improves customer orientation, and supports growth. Platform choice depends on goals: Yandex is strong for Russia and CIS, Google for international projects, Mapbox for custom design, Leaflet and OSM for open lightweight solutions, and Baidu for China.

If you need a ready platform with no extra hosting costs, I can publish the map on GitHub Pages for free or embed it directly into your website.

What I implement:

  • interactive maps of any complexity on Yandex / Leaflet / OSM / Mapbox / Google / Baidu;
  • display of your entities from CRM, Excel, database, or API;
  • filters, categories, layers, and switchable basemaps;
  • clustering for large volumes of points;
  • custom markers, object cards, and popups;
  • routing, geocoding, and address search;
  • mobile adaptation;
  • performance optimization and consistent rendering across devices.

To get started, I need:

  • task description: what users should do on the map and what business outcome you expect;
  • data to display (points, objects, categories, attributes);
  • website access (if the map should be embedded);
  • examples or references, if any;
  • desired style and visual requirements.

If your data is incomplete or poorly formatted, I can help clean and structure it. After receiving materials, I will prepare a map prototype and align the key logic with you.
